# Source attribution

Marvel Unification is a consolidation of Dr-Ake's existing local Marvel mods:

- Cyclops-X-Gene
- Rimworld-Deadpool-healing
- RimWorld-Gambit
- Rimworld-Incredible-Hulk
- Rimworld-Jean-Gray
- Rimworld-Magneto-X-Gene
- Rimworld-Multiple-Man-X-Gene
- Rimworld-Nightcrawler-X-Gene
- Rimworld-Storm

The Deadpool source mod includes the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International license. Its license text is preserved in `LICENSE`. Changes to that module are the unified patch registration, shared injector checks and consumption, and correction of its existing kidnapping-protection check. The import manifest identifies original files and hashes; the source folders remain unchanged.

Original textures and sounds are retained byte for byte. Game assemblies and Harmony are referenced for compilation and are not redistributed in this collection. This notice does not grant additional rights to third-party character names or artwork.
